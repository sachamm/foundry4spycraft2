# Starter cast

The system copies this cast into each world's Actors tab on the first GM launch after installation or upgrade. All 16 sample agents also appear in **Sample PCs**. The elected active GM performs setup; other connected clients do not import a second set. No scene is activated and no tokens are placed.

## Glass Meridian

A shipping consultancy hides a market for stolen identities and diverted cargo. A vanished courier has left behind a ledger that could expose its clients. The agents might recover it, protect the courier or turn the network's divided staff against its owner.

| Folder | Character | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Masterminds | Vera Kestrel — The Glass Broker | Intelligence broker who trades favors and keeps an escape route |
| Henchmen | Anton Rusk — Security Chief | Protective lieutenant whose loyalty depends on what he believes |
| Henchmen | Leila Sayegh — Signal Keeper | Camera and access specialist holding a private backup |
| Henchmen | Milo Vale — Retrieval Leader | Field operator with a rival buyer for the ledger |

These four are special NPCs, with the Mastermind or Henchman quality, full starting vitality and wounds, linked tokens, graded statistics, equipment, feats and original scenario notes. They start at Threat Level 1 and follow the active mission's Threat Level. Set the mission and review current health before play at another Threat Level.

## Minions

The **Minions** folder contains eight reusable standard NPC profiles selected from the Core NPC Templates library: Security Guard, Soldier, Mercenary, Criminal Enforcer, Burglar, Enemy Agent, Driver and Medic. Each has the Minion quality and group equipment pricing. Copy these actors or place multiple unlinked tokens to build a team. A group's vehicle pick provides one shared vehicle, not a separate vehicle for each token.

The templates retain the original library's equipment, feats, portraits and tokens. Special NPC conversions retain compatible qualities and receive their own included ammunition allowance. No rulebook descriptions are included.

## Setup behavior

- Folders: **Masterminds**, **Henchmen**, **Minions**, **Sample PCs**. The old **Sample Level-1 Agents** world folder is reused and renamed.
- Recognizable sample imports are reused and filed during this initial setup, including PCs you renamed or edited; their character data is preserved.
- Setup records each completed import and resumes after an interruption. Once complete, it does not restore deleted samples or undo later folder moves and edits.
- The Scout's animal partner is created and linked through the normal sample-PC companion importer.
- Additional copies remain available from **Sample Villains & Minions** and **Sample Level-1 Agents** in Compendium Packs.

The cast and import lifecycle are covered by automated checks. Test the new setup in a separate world before relying on it in a campaign already in progress.
